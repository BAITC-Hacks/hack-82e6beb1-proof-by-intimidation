# Education product guidance (Kazakhstan)

## Likely users
- School students (grades 5-11), especially preparing for UNT (ЕНТ).
- School teachers, overloaded with lesson planning, grading and reporting.
- University students and advisors.
- Parents tracking progress; school administrators.

## Real pain points to anchor on
- Teacher workload: lesson plans, worksheets, grading, paperwork.
- Uneven quality between urban and rural schools; shortage of subject teachers.
- Trilingual education (Kazakh / Russian / English); materials often missing in one language.
- UNT prep is expensive (private tutors); students need personalised practice.
- Weak feedback loops: students don't learn *why* an answer is wrong.

## What judges will like
- A specific persona and a concrete before/after ("teacher spends 3 hours -> 10 minutes").
- The agent completes a real multi-step job, not just answers questions.
- Measurable impact (time saved, scores, reach).
- Works in Kazakh and Russian, not only English.
- Responsible AI: no invented facts stated as certain, age-appropriate content, minimal student data.

## Sample data
- Always create realistic seed data in `data/` (a student profile, a topic list, a few questions) so the demo never depends on live typing.
- Use invented names, never real students.
